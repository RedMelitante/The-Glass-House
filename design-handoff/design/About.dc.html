Our story

Who is The Glass House?

What makes The Glass House a trusted glass company in Cape Cod?

For more than 30 years, The Glass House has been a family-owned, locally operated glass installation company serving Cape Cod, Southeastern Massachusetts, and select Rhode Island communities. We specialize in custom glass and mirror installations for homeowners, builders, and businesses.

From frameless glass shower enclosures and custom mirrors to commercial storefronts and architectural glass, every project is handled with precision, safety, and design-driven craftsmanship.

What types of glass projects do you handle?

We install frameless shower enclosures, custom mirrors, commercial storefronts, architectural glass systems, glass railings, and custom entryways, all delivered with precision, safety, and design-driven craftsmanship.

“We don’t just install glass, we transform spaces across Cape Cod.”

Why choose The Glass House for glass installation?

Our team combines over 30 years of local experience with modern design expertise, precision fabrication, and clean, professional installations.
