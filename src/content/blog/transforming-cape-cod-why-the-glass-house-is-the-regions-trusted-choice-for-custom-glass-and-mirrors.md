A successful project is built on more than just high-quality glass; it’s built on a meticulous process. The Glass House follows a proven "Concept-to-Completion" framework:

1. **Consultation & Site Measurement:** We assess your space, design goals, and budget to ensure accurate fabrication from the start.
2. **Design & Planning:** Our experts, including seasoned consultants like **Orny Beltrame**, provide clear design guidance and material selection to meet both aesthetic and functional needs.
3. **Industry-Grade Fabrication:** Using advanced cutting, polishing, and tempering techniques, we prepare your glass to the highest industry standards.
4. **Installation & Alignment:** Our experienced installers ensure secure mounting, proper sealing, and perfect hardware placement for long-term durability.
5. **Inspection & Final Touches:** We perform a rigorous final check on fit and performance, leaving your new glass polished and pristine.
6. **Maintenance Support:** We don’t just leave you with a finished product; we provide guidance on how to keep your glass clear, safe, and beautiful for years to come.
