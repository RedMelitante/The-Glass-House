## **What Are Frameless Glass Shower Doors?**

If you’ve been thinking about upgrading your bathroom, you’ve probably heard the term frameless glass shower doors Cape Cod homeowners love—but what does it actually mean?

Frameless glass shower doors are [modern shower enclosures](/custom-glass-shower-doors-enclosures-the-complete-guide/) made from thick, tempered glass panels without heavy metal framing. Instead of bulky borders, they use minimal hardware like small hinges or clips to stay in place.

This creates a clean, open look that feels more like a spa than a traditional bathroom.

Unlike framed shower doors, which have aluminum edges and visible supports, frameless designs focus on simplicity and transparency. That means you get:

- A more spacious-looking bathroom
- Clear, uninterrupted glass panels
- A sleek and modern finish

In Cape Cod homes—where coastal light and airy interiors are popular—this design fits perfectly. It allows natural light to flow freely, making even small bathrooms feel brighter and bigger.

The price of frameless glass shower doors can vary depending on several factors:

- **Door style** – Sliding systems can differ in cost compared to hinged or walk-in panels
