## Plan the Layout Like a Pro: Positioning Your Walk-In Shower for Maximum Impact

Designing a stunning bathroom doesn’t start with tiles or taps—it starts with **layout**. And when it comes to **walk-in shower enclosures**, where you place it can make all the difference.

Start by asking:  
 📍 *Where does it make the most sense for flow and functionality?* 📍 *Can the enclosure make the space feel bigger and brighter?*

Here are some layout tips to keep in mind:

- **Corner installations** work beautifully in compact bathrooms—they save space without sacrificing style.
- **Wall-to-wall walk-ins** are perfect for master bathrooms, offering a wide-open, luxurious look.
- Consider **natural light**—placing your shower near a window (with privacy glass, of course!) can brighten the whole room.
- Don't forget to think about **accessibility** and **drainage** during the early planning phase.

## Choose the Right Glass and Hardware: It’s Not Just a Box, It’s a Statement

Let’s be real—your walk-in shower enclosure isn’t just for function. It’s one of the first things people notice in your bathroom. So why not make it a showstopper?

The beauty of walk-in shower enclosures lies in their customisability. From the type of **glass** to the style of **hardware**, every detail can elevate the design.

Here’s what you’ll want to consider:

- **Glass options**:  
   ▪️ *Clear glass* gives a clean, modern look and makes small spaces feel bigger  
   ▪️ *Frosted or tinted glass* adds privacy with style  
   ▪️ *Textured or patterned glass* can bring personality and soften the look
- **Glass thickness**: Thicker glass doesn’t just feel luxurious—it adds stability and safety too.
- **Hardware finishes**: Whether you're into matte black, brushed nickel, gold, or chrome, the right finish ties everything together and sets the tone of the space.
