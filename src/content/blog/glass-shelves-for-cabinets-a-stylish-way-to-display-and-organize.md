## Where and How to Use Glass Shelves in Cabinets

Glass shelves for cabinets aren’t just for kitchens—though they do shine there. Their versatility makes them a smart upgrade in *almost any* room where cabinets live.

### **Where to Use Them:**

- **Kitchen Cabinets:** Perfect for showcasing glassware, dinnerware, or your favorite mugs.
- **Bathroom Cabinets:** Keep toiletries, skincare, and towels neatly displayed in an elegant, spa-like setup.
- **Living Room Display Units:** Ideal for styling décor, collectibles, or even books with a modern twist.

### **How to Use Them:**

- **Choose the Right Glass:** Tempered glass is the safest and strongest for everyday use. You can also opt for frosted or tinted glass for a softer, more custom look.
- **Mix and Match:** Pair glass shelves with wood or metal frames to add contrast and texture to your design.
- **Create Visual Flow:** Use evenly spaced shelves and avoid overcrowding. Let the items breathe to keep the look clean and refined.

With expert help from **The Glass House**, you can customize your glass shelves to perfectly fit your cabinet space—down to the millimeter.

## Styling Ideas to Make Your Glass Shelves Pop

So, you’ve installed beautiful glass shelves for cabinets—now what? Styling them is where the magic really happens. With the right touch, your cabinets can become a standout feature in your home, not just storage space.

### **Here are a few easy styling tips to try:**

- **Group by Color or Theme:** Whether it’s all-white dishware or a pop of green plants, creating visual harmony keeps the shelves from looking cluttered.
- **Play with Height and Shape:** Mix tall jars with shorter bowls or stack plates beside vertical containers to create visual interest.
- **Add Lighting:** A little LED strip under or inside the cabinet adds an elegant glow that makes glass shelves shine—literally.
- **Use Beautiful Containers:** Transparent jars, ceramic vases, or minimal trays help keep items looking organized and intentional.
- **Keep It Light:** Resist the urge to overfill. A few carefully chosen items can make a bigger impact than a crowded shelf.
