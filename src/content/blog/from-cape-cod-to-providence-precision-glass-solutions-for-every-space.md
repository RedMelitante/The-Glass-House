## Residential Excellence: Elevating the Home

Your home is your sanctuary, and custom glass is one of the most effective ways to enhance its style and light.

### Shower Enclosures: Frameless vs. Framed

We specialize in tailoring shower enclosures to your specific bathroom layout. Frameless shower enclosures offer a minimalist, high-end look that makes small bathrooms feel significantly larger. For those seeking a more traditional aesthetic, our framed enclosures provide structural integrity and classic design.

### Custom Mirrors and Style

A well-placed mirror does more than reflect; it enhances natural light and adds a layer of sophistication to any room. From oversized vanity mirrors to custom-cut decorative pieces, we help you choose the right fit for your interior.

### Specialty Residential Glass

- **[Glass Railings](/glass-services/glass-railings/):** Modernize your staircases, balconies, and pool areas with secure, crystal-clear railings that preserve your views.
- **[Fireplace Screens:](/glass-services/fireplace-screens/)** Our heat-resistant, polished glass screens add safety to your hearth while ensuring the beauty of the fire remains the focal point.
- **[Wine Cellar Enclosures](/glass-services/glass-wine-cellar/):** Create a climate-friendly, elegant display for your wine collection that serves as a stunning conversation piece.
