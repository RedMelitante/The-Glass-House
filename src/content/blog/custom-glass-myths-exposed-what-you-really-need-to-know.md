When it comes to custom glass installations, there are plenty of misconceptions that might make homeowners hesitant to explore this elegant option. Whether you're considering a custom glass shower enclosure, a sleek glass railing, or a modern glass partition, it's important to separate fact from fiction. In this blog, we'll debunk some common myths about custom glass, focusing on durability, safety, and cost.

#### Myth 1: Custom Glass is Fragile and Easily Breakable

**Reality:** One of the most persistent myths about custom glass is that it's delicate and prone to breaking. This couldn't be further from the truth. Modern custom glass, especially when tempered or laminated, is incredibly strong and durable. Tempered glass, for example, is treated through a heating and cooling process that makes it up to four times stronger than regular glass. Laminated glass consists of layers with a plastic interlayer, making it difficult to shatter even under force.

These types of glass are designed to withstand daily wear and tear, including accidental impacts. Whether it's a glass shower door that sees frequent use or a glass railing on a staircase, custom glass is built to last.

#### Myth 2: Custom Glass is Not Safe for Homes

**Reality:** Safety is a top priority when it comes to custom glass installations. As mentioned earlier, most custom glass products used in homes are either tempered or laminated, both of which are engineered for safety.

Tempered glass, when broken, shatters into small, blunt pieces that are less likely to cause injury. Laminated glass, on the other hand, tends to stay intact even if it cracks, as the interlayer holds the pieces together. This feature is particularly beneficial for areas where safety is paramount, like in railings or large windows.

In fact, custom glass can often be a safer choice than traditional materials. Glass panels in staircases or balconies, for example, offer clear sightlines and prevent accidents by allowing unobstructed views of potential hazards.

#### Myth 3: Custom Glass is Too Expensive

**Reality:** While custom glass may have a higher upfront cost compared to some traditional materials, it’s important to consider the long-term value it provides. Custom glass installations are an investment in both aesthetics and functionality. They enhance the look and feel of a space, often increasing property value.

Moreover, custom glass is highly durable and low maintenance, reducing the need for frequent repairs or replacements. When you factor in the longevity and the added elegance it brings to your home, the cost of custom glass becomes much more reasonable.

Additionally, custom glass can be tailored to fit your budget. From selecting different types of glass to choosing various finishes and thicknesses, there are plenty of options to align with your financial plans.

#### Myth 4: Custom Glass is Hard to Maintain

**Reality:** Another common misconception is that custom glass is difficult to maintain and keep clean. In reality, glass is one of the easiest materials to care for. Regular cleaning with a mild glass cleaner and a soft cloth is typically all that's needed to keep it looking pristine.

For areas prone to water spots, like shower enclosures, applying a protective coating can minimize buildup and make cleaning even easier. Plus, custom glass doesn't require the refinishing or repainting that other materials might, saving you time and effort in the long run.

#### Myth 5: Custom Glass is Not Energy Efficient

**Reality:** Many people assume that glass installations lead to higher energy costs due to poor insulation. However, custom glass can be designed with energy efficiency in mind. Double-glazed or insulated glass units (IGUs) provide excellent thermal insulation, helping to maintain indoor temperatures and reduce energy bills.

Low-E (low emissivity) coatings can also be applied to glass to reflect heat while allowing natural light to enter, further enhancing energy efficiency. When used in windows, doors, or partitions, energy-efficient glass contributes to a more sustainable home environment.

#### Conclusion

Custom glass is a versatile and valuable addition to any home, offering both beauty and practicality. By debunking these common myths, we hope to highlight the true benefits of custom glass installations. Whether you're upgrading your bathroom, enhancing your staircase, or simply adding a touch of modern elegance to your space, custom glass is a durable, safe, and cost-effective choice.
