Most homeowners are surprised that it’s not just the glass itself—it’s the details that drive the final cost:

- **Glass type & thickness** (tempered, laminated, low-E)
- **Design complexity** (standard vs fully custom fit)
- **Size of the installation**
- **Hardware & finishes** (hinges, handles, coatings)
- **Labor & installation difficulty**
