- **Flawless Craftsmanship:** Every piece is made-to-measure, ensuring a perfect fit for unique architectural niches.

## Design Options: Personalizing Your Reflection

When you choose a custom mirror, you are the designer. You have the freedom to select from various styles and finishes to complement your existing décor.

### Framed vs. Frameless Designs

- **Framed Designs:** Add a classic or modern border to make the mirror feel like a piece of art.
- **Frameless Designs:** Offer a sleek, minimalist look that blends seamlessly into the wall, perfect for contemporary spaces.

### Mirror Edge and Finish Options

The "finish" of a mirror is where true elegance lies. Options include:

- **Polished Edges:** Smooth and sleek for a modern look.
- **Beveled Edges:** Slanted edges that act as a built-in frame, catching the light beautifully.
- **Decorative Edges:** For a unique, artisan touch.
- **Tinted and Specialty Finishes:** From antique tints to modern smoked glass, these finishes offer a unique style reflection.

## The Professional Installation Process

A custom mirror is only as good as its installation. Professional service in Cape Cod ensures that the process is handled with precision from start to finish.

1. **On-Site Measurements:** Precision is key. Experts take exact measurements of your space to ensure the fabrication is perfect.
2. **Precision Fabrication:** Using advanced cutting techniques, the glass is shaped to your exact specifications.
3. **Secure Mounting:** Professionals use specialized mounting systems for safe wall anchoring. This is especially crucial for large-scale gym mirrors or heavy framed pieces.
4. **Polished Finishing:** The final result is a clean alignment with smooth edge finishing and a flawless, streak-free surface.
